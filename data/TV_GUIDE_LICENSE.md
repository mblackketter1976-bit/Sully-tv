# TV Guide data attribution and license

TVmaze-sourced episode schedules in `tv-guide.json` are adapted from [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/) via its [public schedule API](https://www.tvmaze.com/api).

TVmaze licenses its API data under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Sully TV's adaptations of those data are available under the same license, without additional restrictions. Changes: selected explicitly reviewed networks, normalized timestamps to UTC, combined show/episode titles, excluded missing runtimes, removed expired/duplicate/overlapping entries, and converted the structure to Sully's JSON contract. Source links and source-specific notices are retained in the dataset. No endorsement by TVmaze is implied.

The data describe scheduled episodes, not verified stream playback. They are incomplete: repeats and unlisted programming are not filled in. A network's schedule does not establish rights to rebroadcast its content, and stream feeds can differ. Other separately sourced data and software are not relicensed by this notice.
